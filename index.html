Method: ridge normalisation, adaptive binarisation, Zhang-Suen thinning, crossing-number minutiae extraction (ridge endings and bifurcations), orientation-field singularity detection (core, delta) for Loop / Whorl / Arch pattern, and rotation- and translation-tolerant minutiae pairing. Score = 75% minutiae agreement + 12% pattern type + 8% ridge density + 5% core/delta count. Sample types (patent, plastic, latent) use different background normalisation and contrast thresholds. Reconstruction smooths the orientation field, extends it two blocks beyond the print edge, estimates ridge frequency and applies a Gabor filter to join broken ridges; reconstructed (purple) areas are never counted as evidence. This is a screening aid: the final opinion must come from a qualified fingerprint examiner.
